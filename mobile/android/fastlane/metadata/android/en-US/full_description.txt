Lectio is a note-taking app for lectures. Record a class and write notes as it goes: each note keeps the moment of the recording it belongs to, so later you can tap a note and hear exactly what was being said.

WHILE YOU LISTEN
• Record with the screen off: a notification shows the recording and has a Stop button. A phone call pauses the recording, and it carries on in the same file afterwards.
• Write notes stamped with the moment you started typing.
• Label notes as Important, Review, Task, Definition or Heading.
• Import the lecture's PDF slides, link notes to pages, and draw on them with a pen or highlighter.
• Add photos of the board or a handout, and formulas written in LaTeX with a live preview.

AFTER THE LECTURE
• Play the recording from any note, at 0.5× to 2× speed.
• Transcribe the recording on your phone or tablet with a speech model that is downloaded once; tap any line of the transcript to hear it.
• Search across notes, slides and transcripts.
• Study with a quiz made from your Definition notes, flashcards that come back on a schedule, and an outline and questions written by a small study model that also runs on your device.
• Export a lecture as PDF, Word or Markdown and share it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Your recordings and notes stay on your device. Transcription and the study tools run on your device; the internet is only used to download those models.

ACCESSIBLE
• Works with TalkBack, follows your font size and dark theme, uses your wallpaper colours, and shows the lecture list and the open lecture side by side on tablets.

There are also versions of Lectio for iPhone and iPad, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web.
