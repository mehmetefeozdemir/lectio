Lectio privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. Applies to Lectio for iPhone and iPad, Android, macOS, Windows, Linux and the web.

In short: Lectio has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data. Your recordings and notes stay on your device.

What Lectio stores, and where

Everything you make in Lectio (courses, lectures, recordings, notes, labels, slides, drawings, photos, transcripts, quizzes and flashcards) is saved only on your device, in Lectio's own storage:

  • iPhone and iPad: in the app's private folder. If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device's backup includes it, under your own account with Apple.
  • Android: in the app's private storage. If your phone backs up apps to your Google account, that backup can include it, under your own account with Google.
  • Desktop: in a folder on your computer (for example ~/Library/Application Support/org.Lectio.Lectio on macOS).
  • Web: in your browser's storage for this website (IndexedDB), on your computer only.

Lectio does not upload these files anywhere. Nothing is sent to the developer.

When Lectio uses the internet

  • Downloading models. Transcription and the study tools run on your device with models that Lectio downloads the first time you use them, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Like any website, Hugging Face's servers receive the usual details of a download request, such as your IP address; Lectio sends nothing about you or your notes. The downloaded files are checked against fixed checksums before use.
  • Optional AI providers (desktop and web). In the desktop and web versions you can choose to use an AI service you run or pay for yourself: an Ollama server or an OpenAI-compatible service. Lectio only connects to it if you set it up. When you then ask for an outline, quiz or flashcards, the text of that lecture's notes, slides and transcript is sent to the service you chose, and its own privacy policy applies. The phone apps don't offer this; they use only the model on your device.
  • Sharing. When you export a lecture and share it (for example by email or to a cloud drive), the file goes where you send it, through your device's share sheet.
  • The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub receives the usual details of each page request (see GitHub's privacy statement). Lectio itself adds no cookies or trackers.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only to record a lecture when you tap Record. The recording stays on your device.
  • Camera and photos: only when you choose to add a photo to your notes.
  • Notifications (Android): to show that a recording is running, with a Stop button, while the screen is off.

Children

Lectio collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

  • Delete single notes or lectures in the app (deleted lectures go to the Trash first, then are removed).
  • iPhone and iPad: delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Android: uninstall the app, or open Settings › Apps › Lectio › Storage and choose Clear storage.
  • Desktop: quit Lectio and delete its folder (see the install guide for each system).
  • Web: clear this website's data in your browser's settings.

Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing to request or delete on the developer's side.
