Lectio support

Lectio is a lecture note-taking app: record a class, write notes that are stamped with the moment in the recording, add slides and photos, transcribe the audio on your device, and study from it.

Contact

Email mehmetefecan@gmail.com with your question or problem. Please say which device and system version you use (for example "iPhone 15, iOS 26" or "Pixel 9, Android 16") and what you tried. Replies usually come within a few days.

Getting started

  1. Create a course (for example "Biology 101"), then a lecture in it.
  2. Tap Record. The first time, allow the microphone.
  3. Write notes as the lecture goes. Each note keeps the moment you started typing it; tap its time later to hear that moment again.
  4. Add labels (Important, Review, Task, Definition, Heading), formulas, photos of the board, or the lecture's PDF slides, and draw on the slides.
  5. After the lecture: transcribe it, take a quiz from your Definition notes, review flashcards, or export the lecture as PDF, Word or Markdown.

Questions

Where are my notes? Is there an account?

There is no account. Your lectures stay on your device, in Lectio's own storage. See the privacy policy for details.

Does it keep recording when the screen turns off?

Yes. On iPhone and iPad the recording continues with the screen locked. On Android a "Recording a lecture" notification stays visible while it records, with a Stop button.

What happens if I get a phone call while recording?

The recording pauses during the call and continues in the same file afterwards (tap Resume if it doesn't continue by itself). Notes stay lined up with the audio.

Does transcription send my audio anywhere?

No. Transcription runs on your device. The first time, Lectio downloads the speech model once (about 150 MB for "tiny" or 290 MB for "base"); use Wi-Fi for that. After a transcription, the screen shows how long it took.

How do I keep a copy of a lecture?

Open the lecture and use Export: choose PDF, Word (.docx) or Markdown, then save or send the file with your device's share sheet.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app (iPhone, iPad) or uninstall it (Android); its storage is removed with it. On Android you can also use Settings › Apps › Lectio › Storage › Clear storage.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or newer; Android 8.0 or newer on phones and tablets. There are also versions for macOS, Windows, Linux and the web; see the web version and the project's releases.

Accessibility

Lectio works with VoiceOver and TalkBack, follows your text size (up to 200 %) and dark mode. If something is hard to use with your setup, please write; it will be treated as a bug.
